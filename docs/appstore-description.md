# Chatter — App Store Description

## Subtitle (max 30 characters)

> Private AI agents with tools

## Promotional Text (max 170 characters)

> Build AI agents with their own tools, knowledge, memory and skills — powered by Ollama Cloud with zero data retention. Bring your own API key.

## Description

**Chatter — Your agents. Your tools. Your data.**

Chatter is the AI chat app we built for ourselves — because we wanted frontier-quality answers without handing our conversations to yet another data-hungry platform. Now it's yours too.

Chatter runs on Ollama Cloud with zero data retention: your prompts and answers are processed, streamed back, and gone. Nothing stored server-side, nothing used for training. Just fast inference on powerful open models — and a native, lightweight app on iPhone, iPad, and Mac that stays out of your way.

**Private by design.** No account, no sign-up, no login — enter your API key and start chatting. We collect nothing: no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry. Your chats, agents, and knowledge live on your devices and sync exclusively through your personal iCloud (Apple CloudKit), end-to-end within your own Apple ID. We couldn't read your data if we wanted to — and we don't want to.

**Build your own agents.** Create assistants with their own personality, model, and toolset — a coding expert, a research companion, a home-automation operator. Each agent is exactly as capable (and as locked-down) as you decide.

**Give them tools with MCP.** Connect any Model Context Protocol server — self-hosted on your LAN, over Tailscale, or anywhere else. Your agents can query your databases, control your smart home, or reach your internal APIs. Your infrastructure stays yours.

**Give them knowledge.** Attach curated knowledge bases to any agent — import PDFs or Markdown, and your agent reads exactly the documents it needs, when it needs them.

**Watch them grow.** Agents remember what matters: they keep their own notes about you and your projects across chats, and they can even write down reusable skills — procedures you teach them once and they follow forever. You stay in control: everything they learn is visible, editable, and deletable.

**Everywhere you are.** One app for iOS and macOS, with your agents, chats, and knowledge synced privately through iCloud.

No subscription. No middleman. No tracking. Chatter is a native client for your own Ollama Cloud account — bring your API key, and own your AI.

*Requires an Ollama Cloud API key (ollama.com). Chatter connects directly to Ollama Cloud and to MCP servers you configure; we run no servers of our own.*

## Submission notes (not part of the listing)

- **App Privacy questionnaire:** declare **"Data Not Collected"** — the description and privacy label must match, or review will push back.
- **Privacy policy URL:** point to a publicly hosted copy of `docs/PRIVACY.md` (the GitHub repo may be private — publish on budo-team.ch or GitHub Pages first).
- **"No subscription"** only holds while the app is free or a one-time purchase; remove the line if a subscription is ever introduced.
- Wording is deliberately careful: zero data retention is attributed to Ollama Cloud (their claim, not ours), and CloudKit sync is described as "within your own Apple ID" rather than "end-to-end encrypted" (only true with Advanced Data Protection).
